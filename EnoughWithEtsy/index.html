Enough with Etsy.

Starting December 16, Etsy's updated Terms of Use let Etsy and its partners use your listing photos and descriptions to train AI. There's no simple switch in your shop settings to say no.

We're building the alternative: a marketplace for human makers that will never train AI on your work, and puts that promise in writing.

Save my founding seller spot

What changed

Your work, their training data.

Etsy published new Terms of Use and a new Privacy Policy on October 26, 2026. They take effect December 16. You still own what you upload, but the updated license adds explicit permission for Etsy and its authorized partners to use seller content to develop and train AI.

Meanwhile, the same marketplace is asking you to prove your work is human. Many sellers have decided that trade isn't worth it. If you're one of them, you shouldn't have to give up your customers to protect your craft.

Don't take our word for it: read the reporting, then read Etsy's terms yourself.

The promise

What we're building with you.

  • 01

    Your work never trains AI. Ever.

    Not ours, not a partner's. It will be written into the seller terms as a contract, not tucked into a settings page.

  • 02

    Move your shop in minutes

    Etsy lets you export your listings as a CSV. Upload that file, and your photos, titles, prices and descriptions come with you.

  • 03

    Human-made, and enforced

    Made by you, designed by you, or curated vintage. Mass-produced resale and AI-generated listings stay out.

  • 04

    Fees you can do in your head

    One simple fee structure, published before launch. No surprise ad charges.

  • 05

    Founding sellers have a say in how it's built

    The first sellers on the waitlist get early access, a founding badge and a direct line to the people building it.

Questions

Is this live yet?

Not yet. This is the founding seller waitlist. We're building now and inviting sellers in small groups, in the order they joined. Nothing changes with your Etsy shop until you decide to move.

Do I have to leave Etsy to join?

No. Lots of sellers will run both shops for a while. Joining the waitlist costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

Can I still use AI tools in my own process?

The core promise is about your work never being used to train AI. We'll publish clear listing standards before launch, written with input from founding sellers, so everyone knows where the line is.
